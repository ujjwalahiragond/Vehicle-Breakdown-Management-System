ABOUT

Vehicle Breakdown Assistant

Reliable roadside assistance when you need it most.

M.K. Garage, Jath, Sangli, Maharashtra is dedicated to making your journey safer, easier and worry-free. A vehicle breakdown can happen anytime and anywhere. Our Vehicle Breakdown Assistant helps you quickly request the right roadside service and get assistance when you need it most.

Whether you are facing a mechanical problem, battery failure, flat tyre, fuel emergency, vehicle lockout or towing requirement , M.K. Garage is here to provide dependable roadside support. We focus on quick assistance, reliable service and customer convenience.

With our digital assistance system, customers can easily enter their vehicle details, select the required service, share their location and submit a request. The request can then be tracked using a unique Request ID, making the assistance process simple and transparent.

Your journey matters to us. Our goal is to reduce the stress of unexpected vehicle problems and help you get back on the road with confidence. With M.K. Garage, reliable roadside assistance is just a request away.
